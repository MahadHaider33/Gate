Gate
Windows 10 22H2 or Windows 11, x64.

Setup
1. Extract the ZIP and run Gate.exe.
2. For use in other apps, install VB-CABLE from https://vb-audio.com/Cable/.
   Run its setup as administrator and restart Windows.
3. Select your microphone in Gate.
4. Select CABLE Output as the microphone in Discord or your game.

Controls
- Background Noise Reduction removes noise. 100% uses only processed audio.
- Noise Gate mutes quiet sounds. Lower it if your voice gets cut off.
- Test Microphone plays your processed voice through your listening device.
  Use headphones. Press Stop Test to stop. Nothing is recorded.

The meter runs only during testing. Testing works without VB-CABLE.
Other apps receive your voice without the test running.

Closing the window leaves Gate in the tray. Use the tray menu to pause or exit.
Pausing stops audio sent to other apps.

Gate v0.1.1 adds separate Voice Changer and Soundboard pages alongside microphone cleanup.

### What's new

- Six voice presets: Normal, Deep, High Pitch, Robot, Radio, and Echo, with adjustable intensity and Hear Myself monitoring.
- Voice Effects stays off when you select another preset. Intensity remains adjustable while effects are disabled.
- A soundboard with WAV/MP3 imports and six included starter effects: Air Horn, Drum Roll, Rimshot, Buzzer, Chime, and Sad Trombone.
- Click a sound tile to play or stop it. Starting another sound replaces the current one and closes the sound settings.
- Inline sound renaming and a color emoji picker with search and categories. Starter sounds have matching default emojis.
- Independent microphone and soundboard monitoring, plus interface and playback responsiveness improvements.

### Download and setup

1. Download **Gate-0.1.1-windows-x64.zip** from the release assets.
2. Extract the entire ZIP and run **Gate.exe** inside the Gate folder.
3. Select your microphone and listening device in Gate.
4. To send Gate audio into Discord, games, or other apps, install [VB-CABLE](https://vb-audio.com/Cable/) separately and select **CABLE Output** as that app's microphone.

Windows 10 22H2 or Windows 11, x64. This is a portable, unsigned build.

When updating, exit Gate from its tray menu before replacing the application files. Saved settings and imported sounds remain in your Windows user profile.

### Notes

- Noise reduction cleans microphone audio before voice effects. Soundboard clips bypass both.
- Noise reduction does not reliably remove nearby speakers or TV speech. Raising the noise gate cutoff can also cut off quiet speech.
- Release build and both automated test suites passed. Comprehensive listening, device compatibility, and long-running tests remain manual.

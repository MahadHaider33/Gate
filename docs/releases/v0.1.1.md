- Added a voice changer with 6 voice options.
- Added a soundboard with 6 sounds and support for your own WAV/MP3 files.
- Choose emojis for your sounds, with search.

Download the ZIP, extract it, and open **Gate.exe**. To use Gate in Discord or games, install [VB-CABLE](https://vb-audio.com/Cable/) and select **CABLE Output** as your microphone in those apps.
